Aprendemica Content Publisher

A private web-based content management tool for authorized Aprendemica creators. It helps creators upload their own educational Spanish-language videos to TikTok as drafts. Creators review, edit and publish the content manually in the TikTok app.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

How the application is used

  • The creator selects content that they own or are authorized to use.
  • The application prepares and uploads the video to the creator's own TikTok account as a draft.
  • The creator opens TikTok, reviews the draft, adds music or other edits, and publishes it manually.
  • The application is not intended for public sign-up or use by unrelated third parties.
This application does not publish content without the creator's authorization.
