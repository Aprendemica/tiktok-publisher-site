Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: July 25, 2026

An authorized creator may request deletion of information associated with Aprendemica Content Publisher.

How to request deletion

  1. Send a message through Telegram: @estrella_moretti.
  2. State that the request concerns "Aprendemica Content Publisher data deletion".
  3. Identify the connected TikTok account without sending a password, access token or other secret.

What will be deleted

Subject to legal and security requirements, the operator will remove stored TikTok authorization tokens, connected-account identifiers and technical records that are no longer required.

Revoke access immediately

The creator may also revoke the Application's access directly from their TikTok account settings. Revocation prevents future authorized API actions.

Never send your TikTok password, access token or refresh token in a deletion request.
