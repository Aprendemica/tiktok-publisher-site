Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aprendemica Content Publisher ("the Application") processes information when an authorized creator connects their TikTok account and uploads their own content.

1. Who operates the Application

The Application is operated by Aprendemica for its authorized creators. It is a private internal content-management tool and is not offered as a public consumer service.

2. Information processed

The Application may process:

  • basic TikTok account identifiers returned after authorization;
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens required to perform authorized actions;
  • video files, captions and related publication metadata selected by the creator;
  • technical records needed to complete and troubleshoot uploads.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to:

  • authenticate the authorized creator;
  • upload creator-owned videos to the creator's TikTok account as drafts;
  • show upload status and support error handling;
  • maintain the security and reliability of the Application.

4. Authorization

TikTok account access occurs only after the account holder explicitly authorizes the Application through TikTok's authorization interface. The Application acts only within the permissions granted by the creator.

5. Sharing of information

Personal information is not sold. Information is shared only with service providers necessary to operate the workflow, such as TikTok, Google Drive and the secured automation server, and only to the extent required to perform the requested action.

6. Data storage and security

Access credentials are stored on a protected server and are not displayed publicly. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to limit unauthorized access. No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Retention

Tokens and technical records are retained only while needed for the authorized workflow, security, troubleshooting or legal obligations. They may be deleted when access is revoked or deletion is requested.

8. Your choices and rights

An authorized creator may revoke access through TikTok account settings and may request access, correction or deletion of information associated with the Application.

9. Data deletion requests

Instructions are available on the Data Deletion page.

10. Contact

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Application, applicable law or platform requirements change. The latest version will remain available at this URL.
